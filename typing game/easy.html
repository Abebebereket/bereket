How to Play

Click "Start" to begin.

A random word or sentence will appear on the screen. Start typing the given text as quickly and accurately as possible. If you make a mistake, you must correct it before continuing. Your time will be tracked, and your score will be based on speed and accuracy. Once the time is up, your Words Per Minute and accuracy percentage will be displayed.

Game Rules

✅ Type Exactly as Shown – Spelling, punctuation, and spaces must match the given text.

✅ No Backspacing Penalty – You can fix mistakes, but time keeps running.

✅ Timer Starts When You Begin Typing – No delays!

Scoring System

✅ WPM (Words Per Minute): Measures typing speed.

✅ Accuracy (%): Tracks how many characters were typed correctly.

Game Modes:

✅ Timed Mode: Type as many words as possible in a limited time
